Control

You hold the switch

Clio is built so that the cautious option is always the default, and so that a person can step in at every point.

Default

Dry-run first

Review a week of notes without writing anything. Change one rule, run it again, and compare the two results.

Your choice

Live, or manual close

Let approvable notes close automatically, or send them to a "to close" queue where a person closes them.

Per agency

Your rules, your thresholds

Blocked terms, blocked interventions, reinforcer policy, similarity limit and approval threshold are set for each agency.

Safeguard

Fixable is never automatic

A note with a fixable mismatch is never closed on its own. A person resolves it, then saves and closes.

Safeguard

Changed notes wait

If a note changed in your portal after its review, Clio does not close it. It asks for a new review first.

On the note's page

Edit, reject, re-review

Reviewers can save and close, reject with a suggested message, or run the whole review again with current settings.

Questions

What agencies ask first

Does the AI decide whether a note passes?

No. The AI extracts what the note says. Whether it passes is decided by ten fixed checks, the comparison with the session's data, and your agency's configuration.

Will Clio change anything in our portal?

Not unless you turn live mode on. By default every run is a dry-run: it reviews, scores and sorts, and writes nothing.

What happens when the AI gets it wrong or fails?

A failed or incomplete AI step never counts as approved. The note goes to a group that a person works, with the reason shown.

Can we adjust the rules to our agency?

Yes. Blocked terms, blocked interventions, reinforcer policy, the similarity limit and the approval threshold are set per agency. We recommend changing one thing at a time and comparing two dry-runs.

Does Clio write or correct session notes?

No. It reviews them. Reviewers can edit a note on its page before closing it, and that edit is theirs.

Will it guarantee that our claims are paid?

No software can. Clio helps you find documentation problems earlier; it does not guarantee payer compliance, audit outcomes or payment.
